Time features:
hour - helps the model to learn hourly trends (like high traffic hours or peak industrial activity)
day_of_week - helps the model to learn weekly patterns and differentiate between weekends and weekdays
month - kind of like a seasonal indicator
is_weekend - to give model weekend indicator instead of having it learn meaning of day 5 and 6 on its own

Lag features:
aqi_lag_1h - aqi 1h before (most likely will remain pretty same)
aqi_lag_3h - aqi 3 hrs before (shows short term condition)
aqi_lag_6h - aqi 6 hrs before (useful for detecting changes over large portions of the day)
aqi_lag_12h - aqi 12 hrs before (may help to capture difference in daytime and nighttime conditions)
aqi_lag_24h - aqi 24 hrs before (helpful for detecting daily repeat cycles)
pm2_5_lag_1h - pm is particulate matter concentration. It will help us predict aqi without completely relying on daily cycles
pm2_5_lag_24h - for example aqi may be low currently but if pm is high aqi is likely to increase
pm10_lag_1h
pm10_lag_24h

Rolling features: Rolling features will summarise several recent values for the stated interval
This helps us see short term aqi and pm levels instead of seeing them at a specific time only
aqi_mean_3h
aqi_mean_6h
aqi_mean_12h
aqi_mean_24h
pm2_5_mean_24h
pm10_mean_24h

Change features: Helps to detect if aqi is increasing or decreasing
aqi_change_1h
aqi_change_3h

Targets: This isn't the complete list of targets but for now its okay as it will allow us to see how our 
predictions vary with forecasting distance
aqi_target_1h
aqi_target_6h
aqi_target_24h
aqi_target_48h
aqi_target_72h
